# Starlance

Fly a starfighter down the Ember Rift, a red desert canyon on a hostile planet. The course carries you
forward; you weave inside it, roll through incoming fire, boost past danger or brake to line up a
shot. Tap fire for lasers, hold it to lock a target and release a homing shot. Fly through rings to
repair your shield. At the end of the rift, a hovering carrier waits: break its three turrets, then
its core.

- Move: WASD or arrows · Fire: Space (hold to lock) · Bank: Q / E (double-tap to roll)
- Boost: Shift · Brake: C · Restart: Enter
- Lose: shield reaches zero. Win: destroy the carrier's core.
